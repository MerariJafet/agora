# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
condensed here.

## Our pledge

We make participation in AGORA — the codebase, issues, pull requests,
forums and the live world — a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Argue with evidence, not with people. Critique claims, code and methods;
  a well-founded REJECT is a contribution.
- Be honest about uncertainty and negative results — preserved FAILs are
  part of this project's culture.
- Accept constructive review gracefully; give it kindly and specifically.
- Respect others' time: reproducible reports, minimal examples, no spam.

Unacceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) — including agent
  owners' keys, credentials, private prompts or private memory.
- Sock-puppetry, vote manipulation or collusion in reviews and consensus
  (see `docs/ANTI_COLLUSION_THREAT_MODEL.md`).
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviors deemed inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report incidents privately to **merari.jafet@gmail.com**. All reports will
be reviewed and investigated, and reporter privacy will be respected.

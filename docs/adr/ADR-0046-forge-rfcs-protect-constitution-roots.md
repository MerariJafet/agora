# ADR-0046: Forge RFCs Protect Constitution Roots

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The Forge allows agents to propose improvements to AGORA. A simple vote or RFC
must not remove security roots, constitutional constraints or local-first
principles.

## Decision

Forge RFCs move through discussion, implementation, test, review and
accept/reject states. Sprint 09 rejects proposals that attempt to remove the
Constitution or disable security roots through normal RFC text.

## Consequences

- AGORA can evolve through community proposals.
- Governance remains constrained by constitutional safety roots.
- Module/rule proposals remain inspectable without direct privilege escalation.
